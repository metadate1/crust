## What is in the repository

Tracked files contain:

- Rust, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS source;
- tests and technical documentation; and
- four original CRUST interface images documented in [artwork/PROVENANCE.md](artwork/PROVENANCE.md).

Tracked files do **not** contain:

- a game disc image, ROM, BIOS, or executable;
- extracted NSD or NSF streams;
- retail textures, models, levels, music, dialogue, or sound effects;
- a retail screenshot, recording, replay, save, or card file; or
- a link to download any of those items.

CRUST reads a user's own supported game files locally. The project does not provide those files,
upload them, or grant a right to obtain or use them.

Wasm bundle, hosted playable site, app package, or commercial product. Each form of distribution
can create additional obligations and risk.

The current public-source plan does not include game data or a prepackaged game. Any future hosted
site must keep the same user-supplied-file boundary unless that decision is reviewed and changed
explicitly.

## What remains unresolved

The project has not established an open-source licensing chain for all source-informed work. These
documents disclose that problem; they do not solve it or guarantee that publication is lawful in
every jurisdiction.

A future open-source release would require authority to license every covered contribution. That
could come from compatible written permission, a properly documented replacement of affected
work, or another basis confirmed for the relevant jurisdictions. Every CRUST copyright holder
would also need to agree to the new license.

Until then, keep the all-rights-reserved boundary and do not advertise broader permission.
